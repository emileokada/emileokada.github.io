Graded Kazhdan–Lusztig Atlas

Dan Ciubotaru & Emile Okada

This atlas contains tables of Kazhdan–Lusztig polynomials, Fourier transforms, and microlocal Arthur–ABV packets. The tables record graded nilpotent orbits and their equivariant local systems, orbit closures, and packet membership. They accompany our work on exceptional groups; computations in classical types are also included.

The polynomial calculations use Lusztig’s algorithm for graded Lie algebras. The packet calculations use microlocal vanishing cycles of equivariant perverse sheaves. See About the atlas for the mathematical background, conventions, and citation guidance.
